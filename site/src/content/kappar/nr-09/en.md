This cane with a raptor's claw handle in Amboyna (Pterocarpus indicus) is a burl growth on Narra (Pterocarpus indicus). Amboyna is among the most expensive and sought-after burl woods there are. The name comes from the island of Ambon in Indonesia, where most of this figured wood comes from. Amboyna is hard and difficult to work, but rewarding to polish to a lustre.

The shaft is made of Andaman padouk (Pterocarpus dalbergioides), which grows in the Andaman Islands and India. A hard wood that is moderately difficult to work.

Collar in Red Mallee, camel bone and vulcano.

The size is medium to large.
