This cane has a handle in Burmese teak (Tectona grandis). Unlike plantation-grown teak, this species, also called Myanmar teak, grows wild and slowly. This species is very rare and highly valued for all its good qualities and appearance. Not listed (CITES).

The shaft is made of Australian blackwood (Acacia melanoxylon), which grows in Tasmania and, to a lesser extent, in the southern parts of Australia. Despite the name, the colour is not black wood but a deeper red-brown tone with black streaks. A highly sought-after wood for its decorative qualities.

The collar is moose antler, amboyna, brass and ebony.

The cane is a smaller size, suitable for women.
