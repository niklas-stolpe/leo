This cane has a handle in Curly Mindi (Melia azedarach), which belongs to the mahogany family and is known by many names. The timber is a medium-hard wood and popular for furniture making. It is often grown on plantations in Indonesia and in other parts of Asia.

The shaft is in Mahogany, which comes from the genus (Swietenia). There are a great many subspecies called mahogany. But mahogany originates in Central and South America, where Honduras mahogany has dominated commercially since the 1950s.
