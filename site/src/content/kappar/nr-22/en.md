This cane has a handle in Tiger Myrtle (Nothofagus cunninghamii). What is called Tiger Myrtle is an unusual variety of Myrtle found in the rainforests of Tasmania.

The interesting effects come from fungal attacks that create interesting discolourations in the wood. Tiger Myrtle is rare and prized by craftspeople.

The shaft is made of Southeast Asian Rosewood (Dalbergia latifolia), a relatively light but hard wood. It is found across much of Southeast Asia and has many different local names such as "Sonokeling", "Bombay Blackwood", "Beete" and "Salisal".

The cane is medium/large.
