This cane has a handle in Batik mango (Mangifera indica), a rare and exotic variety of the mango tree. It grows in places such as India and Indonesia, and also in Hawaii, where it originates. The beautiful pattern and colours are formed by what is called "spalting", a fungal attack that occurs in the wood before it dries.

The shaft is in Macassar ebony (Diospyros celebica), a species from the ebony family. A beautiful and valuable wood with lovely colours and stripes. Macassar has very high density and is heavier than most woods.

The collar is in Red Mallee and aluminium.
