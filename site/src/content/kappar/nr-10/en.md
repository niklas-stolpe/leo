This cane has both handle and shaft in masur birch (Betula Pendula var. carelica), which grows mainly in Finland, Russia and the northern parts of Sweden and Norway. It was discovered on the Karelian Isthmus and received its Latin name there. The characteristic appearance arises from a combination of genetic, physiological and environmental factors. Masur birch is a relatively hard wood, but also tough, with high splitting strength.

The collar is a combination of moose antler and Red Mallee.
