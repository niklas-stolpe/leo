This cane has a pistol handle in Amboyna (Pterocarpus indicus), which is a burl on the Narra tree (Pterocarpus indicus). Amboyna is among the most expensive and sought-after burls there are. The name comes from the island of Ambon in Indonesia, where most of this figured wood comes from. Amboyna is hard and difficult to work, but rewarding to polish to a lustre.

The shaft is in Andaman Padauk (Pterocarpus dalbergioides), which grows in the Andaman Islands and India. A hard wood that is moderately difficult to work.

Handle in camel bone and brass.
