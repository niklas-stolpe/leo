This fine cane has a handle in Amboyna (Pterocarpus indicus), which is a burl on the Narra tree (Pterocarpus indicus). Amboyna is among the most expensive and sought-after burls there are. The name comes from the island of Ambon in Indonesia, where most of this figured wood comes from. Amboyna is hard and difficult to work, but rewarding to polish to a lustre.

The shaft is in Macassar ebony (Diospyros celebica), a species from the ebony family. A beautiful and valuable wood with lovely colours and stripes. Macassar has very high density and is heavier than most woods.

The collar is in camel bone and aluminium.
