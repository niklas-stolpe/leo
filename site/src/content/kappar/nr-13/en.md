This cane has a pistol grip in Purple Heart (genus Peltogyne). Its natural habitats are Central and South America. It is sometimes called "Amaranth". The strong, eye-catching purple colour is natural and rewarding to create contrast with. A hard wood that can be a challenge to work with, and whose dust can cause irritation and allergy-like symptoms.

The shaft is made of American walnut (Juglans nigra), which grows in the eastern USA. An exclusive, hard and dimensionally stable wood that can have varying brown tones as its colour.

Collar and decoration are in camel bone and brass.

The cane is a women's model.
