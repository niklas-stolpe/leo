This cane has a handle in Surren wood (Toona sureni), also called Indonesian or Vietnamese mahogany. A large tree that can grow up to 60 metres tall with a diameter of up to 3 metres.

The shaft is made of Swedish flame birch (Betula Pendula), a particularly beautiful variety of silver birch, valued for its wavy, three-dimensional "flamed" pattern in the wood. Not to be confused with masur birch, whose pattern arises from a genetic defect.

The collar is Lignum Vitae and moose antler.
