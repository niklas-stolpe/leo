This cane has both handle and shaft in Batik mango (Mangifera indica), a rare and exotic variety of the mango tree. It grows in places such as India and Indonesia, and also in Hawaii, where it originates. The beautiful pattern and colours are formed by what is called "spalting", a fungal attack that occurs in the wood before it dries.

Collar in ebony and aluminium.
