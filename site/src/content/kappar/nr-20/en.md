This cane has a handle in masur birch (Betula Pendula var. carelica), which grows mainly in Finland, Russia and the northern parts of Sweden and Norway. It was discovered on the Karelian Isthmus and received its Latin name there. The characteristic appearance arises from a combination of genetic, physiological and environmental factors. Masur birch is a relatively hard wood, but also tough, with high splitting strength.

The shaft is made of Southeast Asian Rosewood (Dalbergia latifolia), a relatively light but hard wood. It is found across much of Southeast Asia and has many different local names such as "Sonokeling", "Bombay Blackwood", "Beete" and "Salisal".

Collar in Amboyna, camel bone and vulcano.
