This cane has a handle in Tiger Myrtle (Nothofagus cunninghamii). What is called Tiger Myrtle is an unusual variant of Myrtle that occurs in the rainforests of Tasmania.

The interesting effects come from fungal attack, which creates interesting discolourations in the wood. Tiger Myrtle is rare and prized by craftspeople.

The shaft is made of Andaman Padouk (Pterocarpus dalbergioides), which grows on the Andaman Islands and in India. A hard wood that is moderately difficult to work.
