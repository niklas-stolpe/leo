This cane has a handle in masur birch (Betula pendula var. carelica), which grows mainly in Finland, Russia and the northern parts of Sweden and Norway. It was discovered on the Karelian Isthmus, which gave it its Latin name. The characteristic appearance comes from a combination of genetic, physiological and environmental factors. Masur birch is a relatively hard wood, but also tough, with high splitting strength.

The shaft is in mahogany, which comes from the genus Swietenia. There are a great many subspecies called mahogany. But mahogany originates in Central and South America, where Honduras mahogany has dominated commercially since the 1950s.

Collar in ebony, aluminium and mosaic pin.
