This cane has both shaft and handle in French walnut (Juglans regia). A beautiful and unusually figured pattern, and a collar with mammoth molar, the cheek tooth of the mammoth, which lay buried in the ground for tens of thousands of years. The mammoth molar has widely varying colours, which come from the minerals in the ground where it lay.

The collar is mammoth molar and camel bone.
